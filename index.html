SYNC CAMERA · EDWARDTEACH

Privacy policy

Updated 8 November 2026 · Android 2.0.0

Delete your account or request data deletion

Sync Camera connects two nearby phones so one can control the other phone’s camera. This policy explains how EdwardTeach handles information in the Android app, package com.edwardt.synccamera. The app is intended for people aged 13 and older.

Your camera and photographs

A live camera preview is sent directly to the nearby device you approve. The camera owner must confirm sharing. Compare the pairing code on both phones before accepting. The connection is encrypted. The app shows a sharing indicator and a Stop control; sharing stops when the camera app goes into the background or the phone locks.

Original photographs are saved in the camera phone’s gallery. Sync Camera does not upload photographs or camera previews to the developer, RevenueCat or an app-operated cloud service. It does not send the original photograph to the remote phone. Your phone’s own photo backup settings, such as Google Photos, apply separately. The app does not request microphone access or transmit sound.

Nearby connection and permissions

Camera access is used to take photographs, provide the approved preview and scan pairing QR codes. Bluetooth, nearby-device and Wi-Fi permissions are used to discover and connect the two phones. On Android 10–12, Android may also require location permission for nearby Wi-Fi discovery or connection. The app does not read GPS coordinates, build a location history or send location data to the developer.

Your chosen device name, connection identifiers and pairing information are exchanged with nearby devices for discovery and pairing. Use a device name you are comfortable showing to people nearby. You can revoke permissions in Android settings; the associated features will then be unavailable.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores a randomly generated profile identifier, device name, language and theme preferences, connection hints, recent device names, capture request records, daily shot counts and cached subscription status locally. Capture records contain identifiers, times and outcomes, not a copy of the photograph.

Signing in is optional. If you create or sign in to an account, Firebase Authentication (Google) processes your email address, authentication credentials and account identifier. Cloud Firestore stores your profile name, its update time and confirmed capture records: shot identifiers, day, the other device’s identifier and the capture time. These records synchronize your profile and shot history between your signed-in installations. Photographs and camera previews are never sent to Firebase. Firebase also handles connection and technical information needed to operate these services.

Pro subscriptions and RevenueCat

Google Play processes payments. The app uses RevenueCat to check and restore subscription access. At startup and during subscription checks, the app sends its profile identifier, or its Firebase account identifier when signed in, and purchase-related information to RevenueCat. Relevant information includes purchase tokens and product identifiers, transaction times, subscription state, store currency and locale, and technical app, SDK and operating-system information. Internet services also receive connection information such as an IP address.

This processing supports subscription access, restoration, purchase verification, fraud prevention and subscription reporting. Subscription checks may occur even when you use the free plan. Communications with RevenueCat use encryption in transit. The app does not receive your payment card details. It does not send your device name, photographs or preview to RevenueCat.

We do not sell app data, run advertising or use an advertising identifier in this release. No advertising-attribution integrations are configured. Google and RevenueCat process information under their respective policies: Google privacy policy and RevenueCat privacy policy. Their infrastructure may process information outside your country.

Account deletion, retention and your choices

To request deletion without opening the app, email kymapuk@yahoo.com from your account’s email address with the subject “Sync Camera account deletion”. Say whether you want to delete the whole account or particular data. Do not send your password. We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting information.

Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to remove its local profile and settings. Delete photographs separately from your gallery and any photo backups. Clearing local data does not delete a cloud account. To delete your Firebase account and synchronized profile and capture records, use Profile → Account → Delete account in the app and confirm with your password. Signing out does not delete the cloud account. Removing an account or app data does not cancel a subscription or erase Google Play’s purchase history. Manage or cancel Pro in Google Play subscriptions.

Synchronized account information is retained until you delete your account or request deletion, subject to applicable obligations. Cloud communications use encryption in transit. Deleting your Firebase account does not automatically erase the separate RevenueCat customer record; you can request deletion through the privacy contact below.

Subscription records are retained as needed to operate and restore purchases, answer support requests and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information handled by the developer or through RevenueCat, email the privacy contact below. Include the app name and, for a purchase request, a Google Play order reference if available. We may need to verify ownership. Some records may need to be retained for an active subscription or applicable obligations. Requests concerning information held independently by Google should also be directed to Google.

If you contact support, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond. Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, receive a copy of your data or complain to your data protection authority.

Children and policy changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it. If the app’s information handling changes, we will update this policy and its date.
